Bionic Font Maker

Upload a TTF/OTF font and get a copy where the first letters of every word are bold. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download 

  


  
Notes: only static fonts are supported (no variable fonts). Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters are processed.
    The font's existing GSUB features (ligatures, etc.) are replaced. The reader app must shape text with HarfBuzz
    for the effect to show.
